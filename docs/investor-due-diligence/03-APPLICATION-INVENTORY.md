# Investor Due Diligence — Part 3: Application & Portal Inventory

ITIS delivers a comprehensive multi-portal suite built into a unified monorepo. Each portal is customized for a specific operational role:

---

## 1. Public Portal & Parent Safety Hub (`/`, `/parent`)
- **Parent Portal**: Child live GPS tracking, bus arrival push notifications, authorized pickup QR code generation, medical emergency profiles.
- **Public Website**: National scholar safety stats, government policy compliance, parent sign-up portal.

---

## 2. Educational & School Safety Portal (`/school`)
- **School Admin & Gate Portal**: Real-time RFID student arrival/departure gate logs, visitor QR scanning, class list attendance.
- **Teacher Workspace**: Quick classroom attendance register, emergency classroom SOS panic button.

---

## 3. Command & Emergency Control (C3) (`/command`, `/responder`)
- **National C3 Command Centre**: Live interactive Leaflet/Mapbox GIS telemetry map, active vehicle geofencing alerts, SOS panic escalation queue, computer-aided dispatch (CAD).
- **Emergency Responder App**: SAPS & EMS emergency vehicle navigation, on-scene digital evidence upload, victim medical summary.

---

## 4. Government & Fleet Management (`/government`, `/executive`)
- **Provincial Government Portal**: Vehicle fleet compliance tracking, CSD vendor verification, driver Professional Driving Permit (PrDP) auditing, PFMA expenditure reports.
- **Executive Cabinet Dashboard**: Macro-level provincial safety scorecards, budget utilization analytics, audit logs.

---

## 5. Operations, Logistics & Legal (`/technician`, `/dataroom`, `/supplychain`)
- **Field Technician Portal**: Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) IoT sensor diagnostics, QR hardware provisioning, firmware OTA updates.
- **Manufacturing & Depot Supply Chain**: Unit-level serial tracking from factory to regional depot, RMA repair workflow.
- **Legal & Investor Data Room**: POPIA privacy compliance vault, ISO 27001 audit certificates, CIPC/WIPO patent portfolio.
