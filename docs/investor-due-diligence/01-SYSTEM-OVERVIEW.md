# Investor Due Diligence — Part 1: System Overview

## 1. Executive Summary & Mission

The **Integrated Technology Intelligence & Safety (ITIS) Platform** is South Africa's premier national scholar safety, fleet telematics, and emergency response intelligence ecosystem.

ITIS solves critical national challenges:
- **Scholar Transportation Safety**: Protecting over 2.4 million public school pupils who rely on daily scholar transport.
- **Real-Time Telematics & Geofencing**: Sub-second GPS and CAN-bus telemetry tracking 50,000+ provincial buses.
- **Biometric Wearable Verification**: Dual-frequency RFID and BLE wearable technology verifying boarding/disembarkation.
- **Cad Emergency Response (C3)**: Rapid dispatch integration with SAPS (Police), Metro EMS (Ambulance), and Traffic Police.

## 3. Core Revenue & Procurement Models

1. **SaaS Provincial Subscriptions**: Annual recurring government license per vehicle node (R3,600 / vehicle / year).
2. **Hardware & Depot RMA Procurement**: Unit sale & maintenance of onboard telematic gateways, panic buttons, and RFID gate readers.
3. **Data Room & Value-Added Services**: Premium analytics for insurance underwriters, transport operators, and school governance bodies.
